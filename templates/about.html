Our purpose

Make academic opportunities easier to discover.

FacultyPath brings publicly available recruitment listings together so candidates can scan opportunities, explore institutes, and visit official application pages without searching each site individually.

This portal is an independent discovery tool and is not affiliated with any institute or government recruitment authority.

01

Listings are collected

Institute-specific scrapers check public recruitment pages and update available position details.

02

Refresh runs daily

When the scheduler is running, it attempts a refresh at 2:00 AM local machine time. The page shows the last successful refresh.

03

Official sources come first

Use the source and application links to confirm current eligibility, deadlines, and instructions before applying.

!

Please verify before applying. Institute pages can change, and automated extraction may not capture every update. The official advertisement is authoritative.
